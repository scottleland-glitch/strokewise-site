Golf stats that tell you what to do

Know exactly where your strokes go.

A player improvement app that finds your gaps and fixes them.

  1. Track every round and the strokes you gain and lose.
  2. Confirm the weak spots with quick skill tests.
  3. Spot the gap between where you lose strokes and what you practice.
  4. Share results with your coach.
  5. Plan practice that shifts as your game does.

Round entry

Quick, simple shot entry

A few taps per shot: how far, where it finished, done. Your scorecard and stats are a tap away at any point in the round.

  • Par, yardage and hole index come from the scorecard
  • Works on the course with no signal
Every shot counts

Strokes gained, shot by shot

Strokewise compares every shot with how your benchmark would play it from the same spot, so you see exactly where you gain and lose strokes.

  • Driving, approach, wedges, chipping, bunker and putting
  • Benchmarks: PGA Tour, LPGA, D1 college, scratch
  • Miss patterns, greens maps, putt reads and club numbers
My Game

See your whole game at a glance

Strokes gained and practice time use the same spider chart, with each skill in the same spot. Side by side, it's easy to see when you're losing strokes putting but barely practicing it.

  • The last 30 days, 3 months, a year, or your last few rounds
Strokes lost vs practice time

Where you lose strokes vs where you practice

Each skill has two bars: the share of strokes you lose, and the share of time you practice it. Putting can take most of the strokes you lose and almost none of the practice.

Trends

Where your game is heading

Trends learns how your practice turns into strokes and forecasts your next few weeks, with an honest range that narrows as you play more rounds.

  • "Holding steady around 76", "Improving – about 74 by March"
  • An early warning when one part of your game starts to slip
Practice plan

Practice what will lower your score

A ranked list built from your rounds, your short game tests and your coach's homework. Start at the top whenever you have time. Skip something and it moves up.

  • Fits the time you actually have
  • Keeps every part of your game from fading
  • Sharpens your short game before a big tournament
Coach connect

Your coach sees what you see

Your rounds, tests and practice reach your coach automatically. Athletes who need attention are flagged, so your coach can step in before a small slip becomes a habit.

  • One team code connects you
  • Alerts when a skill slips or practice doesn't match the gaps
